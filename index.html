FAQ

Questions?

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Most of our members started as beginners. You get a free assessment and a trainer shows you every machine on day one.

Is there a joining or registration fee?

A one-time ₹500 registration fee applies to monthly plans. It's waived on quarterly and yearly plans.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Yearly members can freeze for up to 30 days, and quarterly members for 10 days, for travel or medical reasons.

What should I bring?

Clean indoor shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Lockers are free; bring your own lock.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. One-on-one personal training is included in Elite and available as an add-on for other plans.
